The maker behind the workshop

Meet Mohd Rafiq Ahanger

A blacksmith by trade and an inventor by habit. Rafiq Ahanger comes from Sirgufwara village in south Kashmir. He studied up to class 10, took modest jobs, and at the age of 20 started his own workshop to support his family. Alongside everyday fabrication, he builds tools of his own design. His company, Rafiq Innovations Private Limited, was set up in 2017 and appears on the National Innovation Foundation incubation council's records.

His best-known work is a multipurpose carpentry tool made of high carbon steel. One tool converts into up to eight: axe, saw, hammer, chisel, nail remover, sharpener, knife and screwdriver. He made it for farmers and households who carry several tools for small jobs.

    Questions

    Before you ask for a quote

    How do I get a price?

    Use the quote form below or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you need, the size and where it will be fitted. A photo or a sketch helps. We reply with a quote for your job.

    What measurements should I give?

    Width and height, in feet or inches. For a gate, measure the gap between the pillars. For a window grill, measure the frame opening. If you are unsure, send a photo and we will tell you what to measure.

    Can you make something that is not on your list?

    Yes. The workshop makes custom metal work from a sketch or photo, takes on lathe jobs, welding and repairs, and builds its own tools and machines. The multipurpose carpentry tool is one example.

    Do you do fitting and delivery?

    Tell us where the job is when you ask for a quote, and we will confirm fitting and delivery for your location.

    Where is the workshop?

    Rafiq Engineering Works, Nambal, Anantnag, Jammu and Kashmir 192125. Use the Google Maps link in the contact section for directions.

    Who runs the workshop?

    Mohd Rafiq Ahanger, a blacksmith and National Innovation Foundation award winner, leads the workshop with Zahid Rafiq and Bilal Ahmad Ahanger.
