RAFIQ ENGINEERING WORKS — FINAL BRAND GUIDE

APPROVED CREATIVE DIRECTION
REW Structural Monogram — the final identity follows the approved first reference board: angular REW symbol, bold RAFIQ wordmark, compact ENGINEERING WORKS descriptor, orange engineering accent, and the tagline INNOVATION IS OUR PASSION.

COLOURS
Charcoal Black: #1B1B1B
Steel Grey: #6B6B6B
Engineering Orange: #FF6A00
Light Grey: #E5E5E5
White: #FFFFFF

BRAND VALUES
Strength / Precision / Reliability / Capability / Innovation

TYPE
Primary direction: bold geometric sans-serif / Montserrat or Eurostile-style lettering.

LOGO RULES
Use the primary logo on formal materials.
Use the stacked logo where vertical space is available.
Use REW symbol for machinery, social avatars, small labels and compact applications.
Use white/reversed artwork on dark industrial surfaces.
Use black/one-colour artwork for engraving, stamping, cutting, invoices and low-cost production.
Do not add shadows, gradients, bevels or extra icons to the master mark.
Do not alter the orange/black/white relationships.
